03 / FLAGSHIP EXPLORATION

Structura AI:
see the detail.

A concept for helping turn visual observations of infrastructure into structured information that can support expert review.

CONCEPT / IN PROGRESS
THE QUESTION

Could computer vision help organise the early visual screening of infrastructure surfaces?

THE CONCEPT

Explore an image-led workflow that flags visible patterns for further inspection, rather than making final engineering decisions.

RESPONSIBLE AI

Any model output would be a screening aid only. A qualified person must verify findings; the concept does not replace structural inspection.

STATUS

Concept / development planned. Model performance, field validation and deployment are not yet claimed.

06 / SAFETY-ORIENTED ROUTING

SafePath

A prototype exploring how route recommendations might balance travel time with safety-related signals. The comparison includes fastest, safety-weighted and balanced routes.

  • Lighting and pedestrian density
  • CCTV and nearby emergency facilities
  • Road isolation and incident-risk inputs
  • Simulated data clearly separated from verified live information
